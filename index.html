About Me

Hello! I'm a graduate of the Department of Computer Engineering and Informatics (CEID) at the University of Patras (Integrated Master's, 2025, GPA: 7.67/10). I've been passionate about programming since I was young, and over the years I've built experience across full-stack development, game engines, and VR technologies.

I'm currently a Founding Full-Stack Engineer at Vertcode Development, where we build production-ready SaaS products and AI-powered applications for European and American companies. I take projects end-to-end, from architecture and development to deployment. Platforms we've shipped include analyse.net, hodosops.com, and restaurant-catalogue.com.

Previously, I worked at StudyFetch (USA) as a Full-Stack Engineer, where I built AI-powered features using Next.js, React, and MongoDB across the platform (studyfetch.com) and the mobile app, serving 8M+ users. I built the entire accompanying website (bot.studyfetch.com) from scratch, and it's where I grew comfortable taking a project from zero to a finished product.
